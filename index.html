What it does

  • Time translator - energy is power x time. Same food needs the same energy, so a weaker oven cooks proportionally longer. Power levels count too.
  • Wattage water test - heat a measured cup of water, read the temperature rise, get your oven's true output in watts.
  • Cheat sheet - the same instruction translated for every common oven rating, 600 W to 1250 W.
  • Portion scaling - an honest rule of thumb for cooking two meals at once, with check-early advice.
  • Standing time stays put - resting is conduction, not microwaves. It never scales, and MicroMath never touches it.

Why it exists

A 700 W oven needs 57% more time than the 1100 W the package assumes - most people guess, and dinner comes out cold in the middle or lava at the edges. The fix is one multiplication. Nobody should have to do wattage arithmetic hungry.
